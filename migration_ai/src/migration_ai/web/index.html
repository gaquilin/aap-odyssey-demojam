    Welcome to AAP Odyssey

    AI-guided migration from Ansible Automation Platform 2.4 to a newer release (2.5, 2.6 or 2.7) on Red Hat OpenShift AI. Six specialist agents follow every migration through 13 stages: they predict what will fail, explain the results and verify the platform. A person decides and starts every change; no agent writes to AAP.
